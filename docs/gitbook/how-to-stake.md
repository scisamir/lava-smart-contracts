# How to Stake

Staking through Lava takes a few seconds. Here is the full process from start to finish.

## Before you start

- A Cardano-compatible wallet (Eternl, Nami, Vespr, or similar)
- The token you want to stake in your wallet
- A small amount of ADA for transaction fees

## Step-by-step

**1. Connect your wallet**

Go to [lava.finance](https://lava.finance) and click **Connect Wallet** in the top right corner. Select your wallet from the list and approve the connection.

**2. Go to the Stake page**

Click **Stake** in the navigation. You will see the staking card.

**3. Select your token**

In the top section of the staking card, open the token dropdown and select the asset you want to stake. Your available balance will appear automatically.

**4. Enter your amount**

Type the amount you want to stake. You can also hit **Half** to stake 50% of your balance or **Max** to stake the full amount.

The card will show you exactly how many L-Assets you will receive and the current conversion rate.

**5. Confirm and submit**

Click **Stake Now**. Your wallet will ask you to sign the transaction. Approve it.

Your order is now submitted on-chain. A batcher will pick it up and process it, usually within a few minutes. Once processed, the L-Assets will arrive in your wallet.

## What happens on-chain

When you submit a stake, an order UTxO is created with your deposit. An authorized batcher then processes that order in a batch transaction alongside other users. This batching approach keeps transaction costs low and makes the protocol efficient.

You do not need to wait for the batch manually. The L-Assets will simply appear in your wallet once the transaction is confirmed.

## Checking your position

After staking, you can view your position on the **Portfolio** page. It shows your L-Asset holdings, the current exchange rate, and the yield you have earned over time.

## Tips

- If you need to cancel an order before it is batched, go to your open orders and click **Cancel**. The full amount will be returned to your wallet.
- You can stake as many times as you like. Each order is independent.
- There is no minimum stake amount.
