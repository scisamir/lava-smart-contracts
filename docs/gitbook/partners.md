# Partners

Lava works with a range of protocols across the Cardano ecosystem, both as yield sources and as destinations for L-Assets.

## Yield source partners

These protocols are where Lava deploys pooled assets to generate the staking rewards that flow back to L-Asset holders.

### Strike Finance

Strike Finance is a Cardano-native protocol that offers staking for Strike tokens. Lava routes tStrike deposits into Strike's staking contracts and collects the resulting rewards, which flow back to LStrike holders.

### Iagon

Iagon is a decentralized cloud storage and computing network built on Cardano. IAG token holders who stake through Lava earn rewards from Iagon's staking program, which are distributed to L-IAG holders as the exchange rate appreciates.

## DeFi integration partners

These protocols accept L-Assets and allow Lava users to deploy their tokens for additional yield or utility.

### Minswap

Minswap is the leading decentralized exchange on Cardano. L-Assets are tradable on Minswap, and users can provide liquidity in L-Asset pairs to earn trading fees and any active liquidity incentives. Lava also uses Minswap for automated reward token conversions when staking rewards are paid in non-native tokens.

### Splash

Splash is a Cardano DEX and liquidity protocol. L-Assets are supported on Splash, giving users another venue to provide liquidity and earn fees on their holdings.

### Liqwid

Liqwid is Cardano's decentralized lending protocol. L-Assets can be used as collateral on Liqwid, allowing users to borrow ADA or other assets against their staking position without having to exit.

### Circle

Circle provides USD Coin (USDC) infrastructure. Integration with Circle opens up stablecoin-related strategies for L-Asset holders looking to pair or hedge their positions.
