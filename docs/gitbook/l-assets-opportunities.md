# What to Do with L-Assets

Once you have L-Assets in your wallet, you have several options. You can simply hold them and let the exchange rate appreciate, or you can put them to work in other Cardano DeFi protocols for additional yield.

## Hold

The simplest option. Keep your L-Assets in your wallet and do nothing. The exchange rate increases as staking rewards are added to the pool, so your L-Assets are worth more of the underlying token every time rewards are distributed. No claiming, no compounding, no action required.

## Provide liquidity on a DEX

You can pair your L-Assets with another token in a liquidity pool on a Cardano DEX like Minswap or Splash. This earns you trading fees from swaps in that pool on top of the staking yield your L-Assets already carry.

Keep in mind that providing liquidity carries impermanent loss risk if the exchange rate between your two assets diverges significantly.

## Use as collateral

Some Cardano lending protocols, including Liqwid, allow you to use your L-Assets as collateral to borrow other assets. This means you can unlock liquidity against your staked position without selling it.

For example, you could:
- Deposit LStrike as collateral
- Borrow ADA or a stablecoin against it
- Use the borrowed funds elsewhere
- Your LStrike continues appreciating from Strike staking rewards the whole time

## Lava Earn

The **Earn** section of the Lava app shows curated yield farming opportunities for L-Assets. These are third-party integrations that accept L-Assets and offer additional incentives on top of the base staking yield. APYs vary by pool and market conditions.

> Note: Earn opportunities involve third-party protocols. Each carries its own smart contract risk in addition to Lava's own. The app displays a reminder about this when you interact with Earn pools.

## Trade

L-Assets trade on Cardano DEXes. If you want to exit your position immediately without waiting for a redemption to process, you can sell your L-Assets on the open market. The market price of an L-Asset should closely track its redemption value since arbitrageurs will correct significant deviations.
