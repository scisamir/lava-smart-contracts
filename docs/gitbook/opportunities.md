# Opportunities

Lava L-Assets open up a range of ways to put your capital to work across Cardano DeFi. Below is an overview of the main categories and what each involves.

## Liquidity provision

Pair your L-Assets with ADA or a stablecoin and add them to a liquidity pool on a Cardano DEX. You earn a share of every swap fee generated in that pool.

**Where:** Minswap, Splash

**What you earn:** Trading fees plus any liquidity mining incentives the DEX may offer

**What to consider:** Impermanent loss can reduce returns if the L-Asset price diverges significantly from the paired token. Because L-Assets appreciate over time relative to their underlying token, this is something to factor into your calculations before entering a pool.

## Lending and borrowing

Use your L-Assets as collateral to borrow other assets without selling your staking position.

**Where:** Liqwid

**What you earn:** Access to borrowed capital that you can deploy elsewhere

**What to consider:** Borrowing against L-Assets carries liquidation risk. If your collateral value falls below the required threshold, your position can be liquidated. Monitor your health factor and borrow conservatively.

## Yield farming through Lava Earn

The Earn section of the Lava app aggregates curated yield farming opportunities that are compatible with L-Assets. These pools typically offer additional token rewards on top of the base staking yield.

**Where:** Lava app, Earn tab

**What you earn:** Additional token incentives on top of the staking APY already embedded in your L-Assets

**What to consider:** Earn pools involve third-party protocols. Each carries its own risk profile separate from Lava's own smart contracts.

## Trading

L-Assets are listed and tradable on Cardano DEXes. If you want to move in and out of a position quickly or speculate on the L-Asset price, you can trade them like any other token.

**Where:** Minswap, Splash, and other Cardano DEXes that list L-Assets

**What to consider:** The market price of an L-Asset tracks its redemption value closely, but short-term deviations can occur during periods of low liquidity or high volatility. Selling below redemption value means leaving yield on the table.

## Strategy: Stack multiple yield sources

A common approach with liquid staking tokens is to layer multiple yield sources:

1. Stake tokens on Lava to receive L-Assets (earning base staking yield)
2. Deposit L-Assets into a Lava Earn pool (earning farming incentives)
3. Use any farming rewards to purchase more tokens and repeat

Each layer adds complexity and risk, so it is worth understanding each step fully before going deeper.
