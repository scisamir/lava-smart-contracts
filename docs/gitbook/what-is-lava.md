# What is Lava?

Lava is a liquid staking protocol built on Cardano. It lets you put your assets to work and keep them moving at the same time.

When you stake through Lava, you receive **L-Assets** in return. These are liquid tokens that represent your staked position. You can hold them, trade them, use them in other DeFi protocols, or provide liquidity with them while your original deposit continues earning staking rewards in the background.

## The problem Lava solves

Traditional staking on Cardano works well, but your assets are tied up. You earn rewards, but you cannot use those assets for anything else while they are staked.

Lava unlocks that capital. You stake your tokens, receive an L-Asset equivalent, and your original deposit keeps generating yield. The L-Asset you hold grows in value as rewards accrue, and you can use it freely in the meantime.

## How the value grows

Each L-Asset represents a share of a staking pool. As rewards are added to the pool, the exchange rate between the L-Asset and the underlying token increases. This means your L-Asset is always worth more over time relative to the original token, without any action needed on your part.

For example:

- You deposit 100 tStrike and receive 100 LStrike when the rate is 1:1
- After rewards accumulate, the rate shifts to 1 LStrike = 1.05 tStrike
- Your 100 LStrike is now worth 105 tStrike whenever you choose to redeem

## Key properties

- **Non-custodial.** Your position is secured by on-chain smart contracts. No one holds your assets.
- **Liquid.** You can transfer, trade, or deploy your L-Assets at any time.
- **Composable.** L-Assets work with other Cardano DeFi protocols so you can stack yield sources.
- **Audited.** The smart contracts were independently audited by UTxO Company.
